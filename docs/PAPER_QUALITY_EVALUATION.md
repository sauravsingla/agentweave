# Paper-quality evaluation evidence

This note summarizes the evidence generated by `.github/workflows/paper-quality.yml`. The authoritative preregistered protocol is `evaluation/paper-quality-v1.json`, and complete JSON/Markdown results are uploaded as the `paper-quality-evidence` workflow artifact.

## Semantic routing comparison

The already-frozen 499-task General-AgentBench set was used for an explicitly exploratory/post-hoc comparison with stronger zero-shot embedding routers:

| Router | Hit@1 | Wilson 95% CI |
|---|---:|---:|
| Frozen AgentWeave | 15.6% | [12.7%, 19.1%] |
| sentence-transformers/all-MiniLM-L6-v2 | 40.7% | [36.5%, 45.0%] |
| BAAI/bge-small-en-v1.5 | 33.1% | [29.1%, 37.3%] |

Frozen-minus-MiniLM was -25.1 percentage points (paired bootstrap 95% CI [-30.3, -19.8], exact McNemar p=7.71e-19). Frozen-minus-BGE-small was -17.4 percentage points (95% CI [-22.6, -12.2], p=3.77e-10). The artifact retains 938 failure/disagreement rows.

This comparison is routing-only and post-hoc; it is not represented as a new untouched benchmark run.

## Confirmatory controlled outcome study

The hypotheses and 30-seed design were committed before the confirmatory repeated-seed execution. There are 12 workloads per seed and 360 paired observations per strategy.

| Strategy | Completion | Wilson 95% CI | Mean quality | Mean cost | Mean latency | Recovery |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| AgentWeave optimized team | 100.0% | [98.9%, 100.0%] | 0.937 | 0.178 | 39.8 ms | 100.0% |
| Single best agent | 0.0% | [0.0%, 1.1%] | 0.720 | 0.420 | 115.4 ms | n/a |
| Random team | 1.4% | [0.6%, 3.2%] | 0.312 | 0.257 | 53.6 ms | 6.7% |
| Capability-only team | 0.0% | [0.0%, 1.1%] | 0.720 | 0.420 | 115.4 ms | n/a |

H1, H2, and H3 were all supported. Exact paired McNemar p-values for completion were 8.52e-109 vs single-best, 2.73e-107 vs random-team, and 8.52e-109 vs capability-only. The artifact retains 75 failure/recovery rows.

## Evidence boundary

The outcome study physically executes controlled handlers and measures actual task completion, delivered quality, cost, latency, and recovery. The candidate agent catalog, capability proficiencies, cost/latency profiles, and induced failures are synthetic, so these results are not production-user or external benchmark-native task-success claims.
